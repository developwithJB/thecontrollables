# Daily Decision-Making Hierarchy

**Product Rule — The Dashboard by AGB Coaching**

This document defines the 4-tier daily goal hierarchy that governs page layout, AI logic, prompts, and feature prioritization across the entire application.

---

## Core Principle

The app should not ask the user to do too many things every day. It should reduce noise, reduce guilt, reduce friction, and help the user make a better day from where they actually are.

**The Dashboard's daily purpose:**

> Help the user understand the day they are in, make better decisions inside it, and leave with one clear next move.

Or more simply:

> Help the user get clarity, choose a realistic win, and shape the day instead of reacting to it.

---

## The 4-Tier Hierarchy

### TIER 1 — MUST ANSWER (Highest Priority)

These are the most important daily questions. The app should prioritize these first on Today and in the Daily Briefing.

1. **What kind of day is this?** — Day type label (Recovery, Focus, Heavy, etc.)
2. **How am I doing?** — Body state + calendar load + energy reality
3. **What matters most?** — One clear focus or next move

### TIER 2 — STRONG SUPPORT

These support the day but should not overpower Tier 1.

4. **What should I eat?** — Fuel guidance based on body state and schedule
5. **What should I protect?** — Energy, time, focus blocks
6. **What should I simplify?** — Reduce decisions, remove friction

### TIER 3 — CLOSE THE LOOP

Best for end-of-day closure and momentum building.

7. **Did I get a win?** — Daily win tracking, ring completion
8. **What should I adjust tomorrow?** — Forward-looking micro-adjustment

### TIER 4 — NOT DAILY REQUIRED

Meaningful but should not be forced into the primary daily experience.

9. Was I a blessing?
10. Deep reflection
11. Weekly pattern meaning
12. Broader impact storytelling

## Page-by-Page Application

### TODAY (Home)

- **Primary job:** Answer Tier 1 first
- **Secondary:** Support Tier 2
- **Lightly:** Support Tier 3
- **Do NOT** let Tier 4 dominate

Layout order follows the hierarchy:
1. Greeting (orientation)
2. Readiness Bar (Tier 1: How am I doing?)
3. Daily Briefing (Tier 1: What kind of day? What matters most?)
4. Today Actions (Tier 1: What matters most — actionable form)
5. Compact Rings (Tier 1: visual progress check)
6. Fuel Today (Tier 2: What should I eat?)
7. Plan vs Actual (Tier 1/2: schedule reality)
8. Ask Dashboard (utility — always available)
9. Weekly Review Teaser (Tier 4: only Thu–Sun, stays light)
10. Forecast (Tier 3/4: predictive, not daily-critical)

### PLAN (Planner)

Supports:
- What kind of day is this?
- What matters most?
- What should I protect?
- What should I simplify?

### BODY (Wellness)

Supports:
- How am I doing?
- What kind of day is this from a body perspective?
- What should I eat?
- What should I protect physically?

### GROWTH

Supports:
- What matters most internally?
- Did I get a win?
- What should I adjust tomorrow?
- Deeper reflection lives here (not on Today)

### MONEY (Wealth)

Supports:
- What should I protect financially?
- What should I simplify financially?
- What money pressure is relevant today?

## AI & Recommendation Rules

- **Clarity over volume** — One strong recommendation beats many weak ones
- **Relevance over completeness** — Don't show everything; show what matters
- **Directional correctness over over-analysis** — Be useful, not exhaustive
- **No motivational fluff** — Be real, practical, grounded in data

The AI should help the user feel:
- Oriented
- Understood
- Less overwhelmed
- Capable of acting

Avoid:
- Too many cards
- Too many recommendations
- Too much reflection pressure
- Too much motivational language

---

## Prompt / Check-In Rules

- Do not stack too many reflective prompts on Today
- Do not require deep journaling every day
- Do not make every meaningful feature a required daily ritual
- Prefer one clear prompt over many partial prompts
- Prefer one next move over a long list of suggestions

---

## Feature Prioritization Rule

If a feature does not support Tier 1, Tier 2, or Tier 3 in a clear way, it should not dominate the daily experience.

| Priority | Visibility | Examples |
|----------|-----------|----------|
| Tier 1 | Highest | Plan vs Actual, body readiness, calendar load, daily briefing |
| Tier 2 | Supportive | Fuel Today, protection suggestions, simplification |
| Tier 3 | Present but lighter | Daily win tracking, tomorrow adjustment |
| Tier 4 | Optional / weekly / secondary | Blessing, deep reflection, weekly review, impact storytelling |

---

## Wins & Blessings Placement

- **"Did I get a win?"** → Tier 3 (light end-of-day prompt)
- **"Was I a blessing?"** → Tier 4 (optional, occasional, or weekly)
- Blessing should not be a required daily task
- Broader impact enriches the app but must not burden the daily experience
