# Fully Charged: 75 Days — user recap and copy ledger

Status: **draft copy pending independent human/theological review**

This is the canonical copy ledger for the 75-day QA bundle. The product must deliver only a published, independently reviewed version in authenticated mode. Local QA may show these drafts with an explicit QA label.

Every day also requires the same five strict circuits and an explicit server-confirmed closeout:

1. Awareness: Scripture opened, assigned reading completed, and one honest truth recorded.
2. Perspective: prayer practiced, gratitude recorded, Control / Release / Move completed, and one smaller faithful action chosen.
3. Habit: Main Promise named and honestly completed.
4. Wellness: personal nutrition and hydration covenants honored, two safe movement blocks completed, and one outdoor block or approved indoor safety adaptation recorded.
5. Environment: friction removed, tomorrow prepared, and one private service or encouragement action completed.

An incomplete or unclosed local day ends the attempt without deleting its history. Completion records practice; it does not measure salvation, divine approval, human worth, or comparative spiritual maturity.

Gospel-first revision v2: all Awareness readings are in Matthew, Mark, Luke, or John. The three prompts are pastoral guidance, not Scripture quotations. Read each passage in context using your chosen Bible. No translation text is reproduced. This revision remains AI-assisted draft content pending independent theological review; existing customer assignments are unchanged.

## Day 1 — Answer the invitation

- Season: Be With Jesus (1/25)
- Scripture reference: Matthew 4:18-22
