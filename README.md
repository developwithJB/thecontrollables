## What Is The Dashboard?

The Dashboard is a mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA) that brings your entire life into one calm interface. It's not a habit tracker or a todo app — it's a **Life Operating System** that connects your calendar, finances, wellness, and personal growth.

Built around **The Controllables** philosophy (from the book), everything centers on what you can actually control:

| Emoji | Controllable | Focus |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| 🦉 | **Awareness** | Noticing patterns, triggers, and emotional states |
| 🐢 | **Perspective** | How you frame situations and talk to yourself |
| 🦈 | **Habit** | The routines and micro-behaviors you repeat |
| 🛰️ | **Wellness** | Sleep, movement, and nutrition |
| 🚀 | **Environment** | The spaces, people, and inputs around you |

## Getting Started

### Installing the App

The Dashboard is a PWA — install it directly from your browser:

1. **iOS Safari** — Tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen"
2. **Android Chrome** — Tap the ⋮ menu → "Add to Home Screen" (or accept the install prompt)
3. **Desktop Chrome/Edge** — Click the install icon in the address bar

### First Launch

1. **Create an account** — Sign up with email and verify your address
2. **Onboarding** — The app walks you through a brief orientation covering your Mission, your first Snapshot, and how the 5 Controllables work
3. **Season Setup** — Name your first Season (a life-scale arc that groups your Projects)
4. **Start a Snapshot** — Pick a 7-day focus from 36 themed options, or create a custom one

### Daily Usage

1. **Morning** — Open the app, review your Today Actions and Daily Briefing on the Home tab
2. **Throughout the day** — Log meals, complete planner items, check off ring actions
3. **Evening** — Close your daily rings (Notice → Align → Choose → Prove → Charge) and optionally log wellness

## The 3-Layer System

The Dashboard organizes your life into three nested layers:

```
Season (Life-scale arc)
  └─ Project (Month-scale container)
       └─ Snapshot (Weekly pulse)
            └─ Daily Check-In (Today's actions)
```

### Seasons

A Season is the largest container — think "New City, New Start" or "Q1 Focus." Seasons can last weeks to months.

- **Name your Season** with an optional theme and Controllable focus
- **Track Projects** within the Season, each with momentum scores
- **Season Close** — When complete (manually or when `ends_at` passes), see a full recap:
  - Season duration and project cards with momentum bars
  - Aggregate wearable data (average recovery, best week, hardest week)
  - Standout projects (most momentum, most struggled)
  - AI-generated Season reflection paragraph
  - Downloadable Season Certificate
  - CTA to start a new Season

### Projects

Projects are month-scale containers within a Season. Each Project has:

- An emoji and name
- A Controllable focus (optional)
- A momentum score (0–100) based on task completion
- Status tracking (active, paused, completed)
- Wearable data attribution — health metrics are linked to Projects with the most planner blocks each day

### Snapshots

Snapshots are the 7-day weekly rhythm within a Project. They provide:

- Themed daily readings and reflections
- Daily quest actions
- Completion certificates
- Weekly review

## Integrations

### Google Calendar

| Capability | Direction | Details |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **Read events** | Pull | Import calendar events as Planner items, filtered by selected calendars |
| **Write events** | Push | Push Planner time blocks to Google Calendar with proper timezone handling |
| **Sync logs** | Read | Track sync history with imported/updated counts |

**Setup:** Integrations tab → Google Calendar → Authorize via OAuth. Select which calendars to sync. Sync runs automatically on Planner load and can be triggered manually.

### Gmail

| Capability | Direction | Details |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **Inbox summary** | Read-only | Counts unread, starred, and needs-reply messages for Daily OS briefing |

**Setup:** Integrations tab → Gmail → Authorize via OAuth. Read-only — the app never sends emails on your behalf.

### Wearable Devices (WHOOP, Oura, Fitbit)

| Capability | Direction | Details |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **Recovery score** | Read | Daily recovery percentage (WHOOP, Oura) |
| **Sleep data** | Read | Sleep duration in minutes, sleep stages |
| **HRV** | Read | Heart rate variability in milliseconds |
| **Strain / Activity** | Read | Daily strain score (WHOOP), active minutes (Fitbit, Oura) |
| **Steps** | Read | Daily step count (Fitbit, Oura — excluded for WHOOP) |
| **Heart rate** | Read | Average resting heart rate |
| **Project attribution** | Analyze | Health data is attributed to Projects based on Planner activity |

**Setup:** Wellness tab → Connect Wearable → Choose provider → Authorize via OAuth. Data syncs automatically every 4 hours on dashboard load.

**Pricing:** 7-day free trial of full wearable metrics after connecting. After trial, a paid plan is required for continued access.

## Welcome Back Flow

If you've been away for **3+ days**, the app greets you shame-free:

1. **Welcome Screen** — *"Welcome back. You didn't lose anything."*
2. **Optional Focus Reset** — Continue your current Snapshot or start fresh
3. **Dashboard Banner** — *"Coming back counts."*

Missed days are never counted or highlighted. Data, not failure.

---

## Philosophy

### Core Principles

- **Data, Not Failure** — Missed days are never highlighted. Every Snapshot is recorded as proof you started.
- **Today Only** — No catching up. Yesterday is data. Tomorrow doesn't exist yet.
- **Direction, Not Tasks** — Your Mission is a heading, not a checklist.
- **Human-Centric Language** — No "AI vibes." The Dashboard notices patterns, not "adaptive intelligence."
- **Small, imperfect action beats grand plans** — Self-trust is rebuilt through kept promises.
